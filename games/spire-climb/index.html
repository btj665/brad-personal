Spire Climb

Arcane Ascension

Seven towers stand on Kaldwyn, each left behind by a Visage of the Tae'os Pantheon. Present yourself at the base, take the Judgment, and receive an attunement mark. Then climb: monster rooms, trap corridors, puzzles that will not open until somebody understands them, and Judgments that want to know exactly what kind of climber you are. Every floor is generated. Nothing above you has been cleared before.

An unofficial fan game set in the world of Andrew Rowe's Arcane Ascension. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the author.

Step one

Choose a spire

Each spire builds its rooms differently, hoards different treasure, and pays a different reward to anyone who reaches the top. The tier is the licensing board's assessment, and the licensing board is conservative.
