Frequently asked questions

How do I request a refund?

Purchases made through the App Store are processed by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it, open the app, and choose Restore Purchases in its settings.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Choose the subscription and tap Cancel Subscription.

Do your games involve real money?

No. Any coins, chips or credits in our games are for entertainment only, have no cash value, and cannot be redeemed or exchanged for money or prizes.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting an app removes the data it stores on your device. If an app has an account, you can delete it from within the app, or email us and we'll remove it for you. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I found a bug or have an idea.

We'd love to hear it. Send it to bjohnson@baldyshilltop.com — screenshots are always welcome.
