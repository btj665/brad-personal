Frequently asked questions

Do your apps cost anything?

No. Everything we've released so far is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. If an app ever offers purchases through the App Store, they will be processed by Apple, and you can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do your games involve real money?

No. Any coins, chips or credits in our games, including every chip in The Tables, are play money. They are for entertainment only, cannot be bought, have no cash value, and cannot be redeemed or exchanged for money or prizes.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting an app removes the data it stores on your device. To delete a Tables account and its play records, email us from the address on the account and we'll remove it for you. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I found a bug or have an idea.

We'd love to hear it. Send it to bjohnson@baldyshilltop.com — screenshots are always welcome.
