Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Baldy's Hilltop, LLC ("Baldy's Hilltop", "we", "us") handles information in connection with our mobile and desktop applications (the "Apps") and this website (together, the "Services"). Our short version: we collect as little as we can, we never sell your personal information, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information we collect

Information you give us

If you email us or create an account in one of our Apps, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, and the content of your message.

Information stored on your device

Most App data — such as game progress, settings and preferences — is stored locally on your device and, where you've enabled it, in your own iCloud account. We do not have access to that data.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple shares only limited transaction information with us so we can deliver what you purchased.

Diagnostics

If you choose to share crash reports and analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymized crash and performance data. We use it only to find and fix problems.

Each App's App Store listing includes an App Privacy section describing the specific data that App collects. If an App collects anything beyond what is described here, its listing and in-app notices will say so.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the Services
  • To respond to your support requests and messages
  • To keep the Services secure and prevent abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • We do not use your information for third-party advertising.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Services (for example, hosting or email providers), under obligations to protect it and use it only on our behalf; when required by law; or in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

Retention and deletion

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above. Deleting an App removes the data it stores on your device. If an App offers an account, you can delete it from within the App or by emailing us, and we will delete the associated personal information unless we're required to keep it by law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, email bjohnson@baldyshilltop.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Children

Our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work hard to keep your information safe.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the effective date above and, for significant changes, provide notice in the Apps or on this website.
