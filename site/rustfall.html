Reporting a problem

The game can write a report for you. Tap the ☰ menu button at the top right, choose REPORT A PROBLEM, add a note about what went wrong, then tap COPY REPORT and paste it into your email. The report lists your build, where you are and what is nearby, which is usually everything we need. A screenshot helps too.

Frequently asked questions

Will I lose my progress if I close the app?

No. Rustfall saves automatically as you play. Close it whenever you like and you'll pick up where you left off.

How do I move my saves to a new iPhone?

Your saves are copied to your own iCloud account. Sign in to the same Apple Account on the new phone, make sure iCloud is on, and install Rustfall. Your saves appear the first time it opens.

Can I keep more than one game going?

Yes. Rustfall has three save slots. Open SAVE SLOTS on the title screen to switch between them or start a fresh run in an empty one.

The text is too small to read.

Open the ☰ menu, tap SETTINGS, and pick a bigger option under Text and interface size. LARGEST makes everything about 40% bigger.

Can I turn off vibration?

Yes. There's a VIBRATION switch in the ☰ menu.

Does Rustfall cost anything after I download it?

No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. The scrip, chips and crates in the game are in-game currency only. They can't be bought and have no cash value.

Does Rustfall collect my data?

No. Rustfall has no analytics, tracking or account. See the Rustfall section of our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my saves?

Deleting the app removes the saves on your iPhone. To stop copying them to iCloud, open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, and turn Rustfall off.
